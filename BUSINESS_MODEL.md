# Business model

## What is being sold

NextDue is not a reminder app and should not be sold as “AI-generated compliance.” Its paid value is a maintained, source-backed country catalog connected to a household model that understands people, possessions, and obligations across jurisdictions.

> Know what may apply, who it belongs to, where it applies, and when to act.

The strongest buying moment is a life transition: moving country, receiving a residence status, buying cross-border property, registering a vehicle, having a child, or starting self-employment.

## Initial segment

Start with Russian-speaking cross-border households, particularly Russia ↔ Uzbekistan. Expand only after the catalog review workflow is proven: Kazakhstan, Georgia, Armenia, UAE, and common EU destinations.

The highest-value early users are:

- families with several citizenships or residences;
- recent movers and people relocating in the next 3–12 months;
- cross-border property and vehicle owners;
- people managing dependant documents;
- self-employed users with recurring reviews.

## Pricing hypotheses

These are experiments, not final prices.

| Offer | Price hypothesis | Included |
|---|---:|---|
| Free | $0 | One person, one country pack, ten obligations, custom dates, `.ics` export |
| Plus | $5.99/month or $49/year | Unlimited countries and possessions, sync, delivery status, catalog-change alerts |
| Family | $9.99/month or $89/year | Five people, shared household, ownership, assignment, family feeds and history |
| Move Pack | $19 once for six months | One origin/destination pair, relocation pack, calendar export; credit toward annual |
| Partner | $2–4 per active user/month with annual minimum | Relocation, employer, university, bank, insurer, or property-manager distribution |

Do not charge for basic custom reminders. Charge for maintained packs, cross-device reliability, multi-person collaboration, delivery, and catalog updates.

## Pricing tests

1. Test $49 versus $59 annual Plus.
2. Compare a useful permanent free tier with a 14-day full trial.
3. Test the $19 Move Pack with the purchase credited toward annual.
4. Gate Family by people and collaboration, not arbitrary document counts.
5. Pre-sell founding annual plans before building expensive notification infrastructure.

## Cost structure

The expensive part is editorial operations, not AI or static hosting:

- jurisdiction research and legal-adjacent review;
- translation and localization;
- source monitoring and catalog change review;
- support for ambiguous applicability;
- notification delivery and calendar OAuth maintenance;
- authentication, backups, auditing, deletion, payments, VAT, and refunds.

Catalog content must remain deterministic and human-reviewed. AI may assist internal drafting but cannot publish obligations automatically.

## Trust constraints

Never claim “complete compliance,” “never miss a legal deadline,” “official government service,” or “AI knows every required document.”

Use:

- “maintained starter pack based on your situation”;
- “official sources and review dates shown”;
- “you confirm what applies”;
- “one plan for dates across countries.”

No sale or partner exposure of household profile data. Partner reporting is aggregate only.
