# NextDue promotion playbook

## Market wedge

Launch with one concrete cross-border problem: Russian-speaking people and families who live, move, work, study, own property, or manage vehicles and documents across Russia and Uzbekistan.

Position NextDue as:

> The trusted life-admin calendar for people, documents, possessions, and obligations across countries.

The core message is not “AI generates a plan.” It is:

> Know what may apply, who it belongs to, where it applies, and when to act.

NextDue should feel like a maintained personal operations layer. It matches source-backed country rules to explicit user facts; the user confirms applicability and exact dates.

### Proof points used in promotion

- Separate country and owner for every person, possession, document, and obligation.
- Official-source link and last-reviewed date for catalog entries.
- User confirmation before an item becomes part of the plan.
- Calendar-ready reminders and preparation dates.
- No passport numbers, tax IDs, addresses, or scans required.
- Russian, Uzbek, and English product/content path.

## Priority audiences

| Priority | Audience | Trigger | Message | Commercial path |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Moving between Russia and Uzbekistan in the next 12 months | A move, job, study, marriage, or family reunion | “Turn a complicated move into one reviewed timeline.” | Move Pack → annual Plus |
| 2 | Recently relocated residents and foreign nationals | Permits, registration, tax, document renewals | “Keep the next official action visible before it becomes urgent.” | Plus annual |
| 3 | Multi-country families | Children, dependants, several citizenships/residences | “One plan, separated by person and country.” | Family annual |
| 4 | Cross-border property and vehicle owners | Annual taxes, insurance, registration, inspection | “Track each asset in the country where it exists.” | Plus or Family |
| 5 | Self-employed people and small business owners | Recurring reviews and filings | “A source-backed schedule, without pretending to file for you.” | Pro discovery; partner-led first |

Avoid broad “everyone has deadlines” promotion until the cross-border wedge demonstrates retention.

## Channel priority

1. **Telegram communities and creators** — fastest access to concentrated Russia–Uzbekistan relocation demand.
2. **Expert and service partners** — relocation agencies, immigration specialists, property managers, universities, employers, banks, and insurers.
3. **High-intent multilingual SEO** — durable acquisition from exact deadline/document questions.
4. **Product referrals** — family invitations and shareable non-personal country packs.
5. **Creator video and webinars** — explain scenarios and build trust.
6. **Paid search** — only after organic landing pages demonstrate activation and paid conversion.
7. **Broad launch platforms and display/social ads** — low priority for this wedge.

## 30/60/90-day execution calendar

### Days 1–30: validate the problem and recruit the beta

Product and research:

- Interview 20 people across movers, recent relocators, families, and asset owners.
- Build plans manually with at least 10 interviewees; record which suggested obligations they accept, reject, or add.
- Recruit two independent subject-matter reviewers, one for each jurisdiction.
- Define the first 20 high-frequency catalog entries and their source/review workflow.
- Create a correction channel for “outdated” and “does not apply.”

Acquisition:

- Shortlist 20 Telegram communities; contact 8 community owners.
- Shortlist 15 experts/creators; invite 5 to a review call.
- Approach 8 service partners; aim for 2 closed-beta partners.
- Publish one transparent “how the catalog is reviewed” page.
- Prepare a waitlist with scenario, country pair, family size, and desired outcome—not document identifiers.

Launch assets:

- Interactive Russia–Uzbekistan sample scenario.
- Two country-pack preview pages.
- One downloadable moving checklist with no personal data.
- 90-second demo video.
- Privacy/source methodology page.
- Founding-user offer and feedback agreement.

Gate to days 31–60:

- At least 40 qualified waitlist signups.
- At least 10 completed plan-building interviews.
- At least 2 communities or partners agree to distribute the beta.
- At least 60% of interviewees say the country/owner model solves a real current problem.

### Days 31–60: closed beta and evidence collection

- Onboard 50–100 households through invitation codes.
- Run weekly office hours in Russian; add Uzbek sessions when the reviewer/support path is ready.
- Publish 8–12 source-backed high-intent pages.
- Distribute two partner-specific pack links.
- Test one Move Pack price and one annual price.
- Collect structured feedback after the third confirmed obligation and after the first calendar export.
- Ask for testimonials only after users have acted on a real upcoming date.
- Publish a changelog showing corrected catalog entries and review dates.

Gate to days 61–90:

- 60%+ onboarding completion.
- 45%+ confirm at least three items.
- 35%+ export/connect a calendar.
- 25%+ of invited households return within 30 days or have a future action scheduled.
- Fewer than 3% of accepted suggestions generate a verified “wrong applicability” report; every report receives triage.
- At least 10 paid founding customers or 5% paid conversion from an eligible beta cohort.

### Days 61–90: public wedge launch

- Publish 15–25 total source-backed pages across Russian, Uzbek, and English.
- Launch through 3–5 selected Telegram communities over two weeks, not simultaneously.
- Release Individual, Family, and Move Pack offers.
- Run two creator demos based on real, anonymized scenarios.
- Start one relocation-agency pilot and one employer/university pilot.
- Add the referral loop after activation metrics are stable.
- Test paid search only on exact high-intent queries whose organic pages already convert.
- Publish a monthly catalog coverage/review report.

Success at day 90:

- 300+ qualified accounts.
- 100+ activated plans with three confirmed items.
- 25+ paying households.
- At least 60% of paid purchases are annual or family.
- Two repeatable acquisition channels with payback hypotheses.
- One partner cohort with activation comparable to direct acquisition.

## Referral loop

The referral loop begins only after a user has confirmed at least three items or exported a calendar.

1. User activates a meaningful plan.
2. Product offers either:
   - “Invite a family member to manage this household,” or
   - “Share these Russia–Uzbekistan country rules.”
3. Shared pack contains no personal dates or document details.
4. Invitee confirms three items.
5. Both households receive one month of Plus or an equivalent annual-plan credit.

Track:

- share prompt shown → link created;
- link opened → onboarding started;
- onboarding started → three items confirmed;
- invited household → paid conversion;
- abuse rate and self-referral patterns.

Pass gate: 8%+ of activated users create an invite and 25%+ of invited users confirm three items. Below that, fix the shared value before increasing the reward.

## Paid-channel guardrails

- Do not buy broad “reminder app,” “calendar,” or generic productivity traffic.
- Start only with exact relocation/document-intent search queries.
- Every ad must land on a matching reviewed scenario page, not the generic homepage.
- Exclude queries seeking government portals, emergency legal help, document forgery, or guaranteed eligibility decisions.
- Never use fear claims such as “avoid deportation” or “never miss a legal deadline.”
- Do not retarget users based on sensitive document, migration, tax, family, or possession attributes.
- Do not upload personal-plan audiences to ad platforms.
- Pause a keyword if the landing page produces under 8% pack starts after 200 qualified visits.
- Require an activation cost compatible with a 12-month gross-profit payback before scaling.
- Cap early paid spend at 10–15% of the launch budget until free-to-paid conversion is observed, not inferred.

## Experiment backlog

| Experiment | Primary metric | Pass | Fail / next action |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Hero: generic deadline manager vs cross-border scenario | Pack-start rate | Scenario improves by 20%+ | Keep clearer scenario copy; interview visitors |
| Preview before sign-in vs OAuth first | Onboarding start/completion | Preview raises completion without lower data quality | Remove preview friction or shorten it |
| Move Pack at $19 | Purchase rate among high-intent movers | 5%+ | Test $9 deposit or annual trial |
| Annual Plus at $49 vs $59 | Revenue per eligible visitor | $59 does not reduce revenue by >10% | Keep $49 and test value proof |
| Free country pack vs 14-day Plus trial | 30-day activation and paid conversion | Higher paid users per 100 visitors | Preserve permanently useful free tier |
| Telegram expert post vs paid placement | Activated plans per 1,000 views | Expert post wins by 30%+ | Rewrite placement around scenario proof |
| Partner co-branded pack | Three-item activation | 45%+ | Retrain partner, simplify referral landing |
| Russian-only onboarding vs language choice first | Completion rate | Language choice causes <5% drop | Detect browser language and defer choice |
| Source/review proof near CTA | Pack starts and trust survey | No conversion loss, trust +15% | Simplify proof presentation, never remove it |
| Family referral reward | Confirmed invited plans | 25%+ invite activation | Change shared object before increasing reward |
| SEO page “add pack” inline CTA | Pack starts from organic | 10%+ | Align CTA with query intent |
| Creator webinar | Activated plans per attendee | 20%+ | Use shorter demo and narrower scenario |

For every experiment, predefine cohort, minimum sample, duration, and stopping rule. Do not declare a winner from fewer than 100 qualified visitors per variant unless the effect is extreme and supported by qualitative evidence.

## Claims and privacy constraints

Never claim:

- “Complete compliance.”
- “Every document you need.”
- “Never miss a legal deadline.”
- “Official government service.”
- “AI knows what applies to you.”
- Guaranteed visa, residence, tax, or registration outcomes.

Approved claim pattern:

- “Country rules matched to your situation.”
- “Official sources linked and review dates shown.”
- “You confirm what applies before it enters your plan.”
- “One timeline for people, possessions, and dates across countries.”
- “No passport numbers or scans required.”

Marketing and partner rules:

- Do not sell, rent, or share profile/plan data for advertising.
- Do not expose individual plan data to partners; aggregate analytics require minimum cohort thresholds.
- Do not use migration status, citizenship, tax, family, or possession data for behavioral advertising.
- Collect only the minimum contact and product-usage data needed for the stated purpose.
- Provide export, deletion, and consent withdrawal.
- Separate transactional reminders from promotional email consent.
- Show notification delivery status; never imply delivery is guaranteed.
- Every catalog item needs jurisdiction, applicability, source, catalog version, and review date.
- Keep corrections visible through a changelog.
- Legal and privacy review is required before public paid acquisition in each jurisdiction.

## Weekly operating dashboard

Acquisition:

- qualified visitors by channel and language;
- pack starts and cost per start;
- partner/creator link performance;
- organic impressions and clicks by cluster.

Activation:

- onboarding completion;
- users confirming three items;
- calendar export/connect;
- items rejected as not applicable;
- verified catalog error reports.

Revenue:

- eligible free-to-paid conversion;
- Move Pack to annual upgrade;
- annual share;
- revenue per qualified visitor;
- CAC and modeled 12-month gross profit.

Retention and trust:

- households with a future scheduled action;
- 30/90-day return or reminder interaction;
- delivery success;
- catalog correction response time;
- deletion/export completion;
- support contacts by catalog entry and jurisdiction.

The product is successful when it is used infrequently but trusted at important moments. Daily active usage is not a primary goal.
